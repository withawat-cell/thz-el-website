Research

Research clusters

Each cluster reflects a broad, ongoing area of activity rather than a single project or paper — drawing on methods from both microwave engineering and optical science to form a cohesive research ecosystem.

Terahertz Components

Metasurfaces for full control of free-space waves

Planar arrays of sub-wavelength resonators that give strong control over the amplitude, phase, and polarisation of terahertz waves — capabilities especially valuable where natural materials are scarce. We apply this to beamforming, polarisation control, sensing, and dynamic manipulation.

High-efficiency broadband terahertz antennas & lenses

Beamforming devices designed around bandwidth, efficiency, directivity, compactness, and fabrication complexity — capitalising on the vast bandwidth available at terahertz frequencies while managing rising ohmic loss.

Substrateless integrated platform for terahertz frontends

A shift from free-space optics to terahertz integration, hindered by high material losses. Our substrateless high-resistivity-silicon platform uses effective medium theory to reach near-zero dissipation and fractional bandwidth exceeding 40%.

Terahertz Systems

Quadrature homodyne detection

Systems drawing on both photonic and microwave domains, spanning heterodyne and homodyne architectures built for robustness and rapid response. Quadrature homodyne detection is our core strategy for the highest sensitivity in terahertz sensing — bridging high-performance components and field-ready applications.

Terahertz Applications

6G communications and beyond

Terahertz spectrum eases congestion at lower microwave and mm-wave bands, in theory supporting Tbit/s links over several kilometres. While line-of-sight limits terahertz as a mobile replacement, it is vital for dense urban base stations, last-mile links, data centres, and aircraft–satellite in-flight connectivity.

Non-destructive evaluation

Terahertz waves penetrate dry, non-metallic materials — plastics, paper, cloth, building materials — with sub-millimetre spatial and depth resolution. We apply optical and microwave imaging techniques to quality control, security screening, gesture recognition, and medical diagnosis across agriculture, wine, healthcare, and defence.

Spectroscopy

The terahertz band hosts rotational, vibrational, lattice, and plasma resonances distinct to different materials. We combine spectroscopic techniques with precision metrology for high-fidelity material characterisation, sensing, and diagnostics.

Astronomy and atmospheric sensing

Water vapour, ozone, carbon monoxide, and complex organics show their strongest rotational transitions in the terahertz band — tracing chemical abundance, temperature, and dynamics in planetary atmospheres and star-forming regions alike. Progress depends on low-loss beam-control structures and high-responsivity detectors built for the terahertz regime.
